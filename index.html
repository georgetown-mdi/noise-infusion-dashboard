Disclosure Risk Demos
Interactive demonstrations of statistical privacy vulnerabilities

Data suppression rules protect privacy by hiding cells with small counts — but they don't prevent every attack. These demos show two techniques that recover individual-level information from publicly accessible dashboards, even when every published value passes the suppression threshold.

All data shown is synthetic. District codes, participant counts, wages, and assessment scores are fabricated to illustrate the attack patterns. No real individual's information is at risk.
Demo 1 · Back-Calculation Attack
Workforce Earnings Data

Select-All Filter Weakness

A user submits two valid queries — both above the suppression threshold — then subtracts the aggregates to isolate a single participant's exact quarterly wage. No cells are suppressed. No rules are broken.

Demo 2 · Complementary Disclosure
Early Childhood Assessment Data

Cross-Breakdown Inference

When a district reports results for most subgroups, the suppressed cell is recoverable by subtraction from the published total. This demo shows how suppression fails when breakdowns aren't coordinated.
